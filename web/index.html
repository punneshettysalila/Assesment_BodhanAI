THE JOURNEY OF A CHAPTER

Careful at every step.
Honest about the uncertain.

Deterministic checks lead the way. Bodhan models handle OCR, translation and speech. An optional LLM is limited to guarded decisions—it does not write Indian-language text.

✓

Doubtful OCR, failed checks, retries and degraded audio are recorded rather than presented as successful results.
